# NEXUS RURAL — Launch Video, multi-shot narrative piece with internal cuts across three locations and characters.

A 1-minute social media video to encourage young innovators from rural communities across Latin America to apply for the award. Voice-over narration and initial structure was already defined, but more recognizable characters were introduced to give the piece a stronger narrative direction.

## CHARACTERS

**@FemaleLead** = A brown-skinned woman in her early 30s who manages a greenhouse in a Caribbean rainforest.

**@MaleLead1** = A young Black man in his early 20s who builds agricultural drones for farming in the Altiplano.

**@MaleLead2** = A light-skinned man in his 30s developing a logistics app in the southern Andes.

## LIGHT AND COLOR LOGIC

Each location was given its own lighting to reinforce the geographic diversity. The Caribbean environment uses cooler, softer tones inspired by the humid atmosphere of a tropical rainforest, while the Altiplano is defined by clear skies, harder sunlight, and higher contrast. The third setting gradually shifts toward warmer, golden-hour light, suggesting the beginning of a new day and reinforcing the idea of a new generation of rural innovators.

This creates a progression from cooler, more isolated portraits toward warmer and more communal imagery as the characters come together.

Technology is treated differently: electric-blue holograms, interface elements, and light traces are intentionally more saturated and artificial, creating a contrast with the greens, earth tones, and natural textures of the landscapes.

## CAMERA

People are framed as part of their environment, not apart from it. Medium and wide compositions establish both the individual and the landscape they work within. 

Wider aerial and environmental shots are used to expand the scale and connect individual stories to the broader rural ecosystem.

Movement remains simple and motivated, favoring slow pushes, tracking shots, and subtle camera drift over highly stylized motion. This keeps the imagery grounded and gives the technological overlays enough visual space to remain readable. 

## OUTLINE

* **0.0 to 18.0 — Three territories, three innovators**
  Environmental portraits introduce each protagonist through their landscape and field of work, moving between medium character shots, wider geography, and practical details.

* **18.0 to 33.0 — Convergence**
  The three stories come together through shared frames, digital interfaces, and the Nexus Rural identity, shifting the film from individual innovation toward a connected network.

* **33.0 to 47.0 — Technology within the landscape**
  Forward-moving agricultural imagery combines crops, infrastructure, and digital overlays, presenting technology as something embedded in rural systems rather than separate from them.

* **47.0 to 62.0 — Community and opportunity**
  Wider scenes expand the focus from individual innovators to people, collaboration, and local ecosystems, ending on a more human and relational note.

* **62.0 to 68.0 — End card**
  Static branded frame with Nexus Rural, FIDA, and Procasur identities.
